About Thyrocare Diagnostic Lab

Thyrocare Diagnostic Lab | Blood Test Centre – Ranchi provides diagnostic laboratory and blood testing services in Lalpur, Ranchi, Jharkhand. Conveniently situated at Lalpur Super Market, Chowk, beside New Rajasthan Hotel in Burdwan Compound, the centre offers accessible blood test categories and preventive health checkup packages for residents across Ranchi.

Patients can visit the centre during opening hours for essential blood sugar tests, thyroid and hormone evaluations, organ profiles, vitamin checks, and Aarogyam health packages, or contact the centre directly by phone or WhatsApp to confirm test availability, pricing, and visit details before booking.
